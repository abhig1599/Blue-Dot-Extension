Blue DotImages for Figma

Keep real images when you copy to Figma

Many sites load images from another domain. The page is not allowed to read those, so Blue Dot puts a placeholder in their place.

  • Allow it once and Blue Dot downloads those images itself when you copy.
  • Images are only read when you press Copy to Figma, and nothing leaves your browser except to your clipboard.
  • You can remove it any time in chrome://extensions › Blue Dot › Site access.
